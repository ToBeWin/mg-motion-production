Create ONE premium motion-design styleframe, 16:9 wide, for an ORIGINAL fictional creative brand AUREL. This is an art-direction concept for a film, not a website, slide, moodboard, infographic, product mockup or collage.
Design idea: typography becomes an architectural space. A monumental, custom capital A, with a broad triangular counter and a dramatically sliced crossbar, is built from three sculptural ribbons. Rich ultramarine/cobalt enamel broad faces, very fine satin aluminum bevels and precisely machined white side surfaces. The A floats obliquely across the right two thirds of the image; its large angled legs extend beyond the upper and right edges. Its triangular negative space creates an unmistakable visual focal point. A thin acid-citron rectangular blade passes through the counter as the only small accent. It must read as an intentional bespoke typographic sculpture, not a consumer gadget, stacked cards, lenses, glass panes, a podium, or a generic torus.
Composition: daring editorial asymmetry, close wide-angle framing, strong depth, huge scale difference, active cropped silhouette balanced with a calm ivory negative space at lower left. Place the exact uppercase word AUREL in a compact bold modern Swiss grotesk at upper left. At lower left place large bold typography exactly 'SHIFT' on one line and 'YOUR VIEW.' on the next, with deliberately designed kerning. A few tiny precise registration marks and one hairline are allowed; no decorative grids, no UI cards, no fake metrics, no chapter numbers. Typography should be genuinely integrated with the sculptural composition.
Palette: chalk ivory #F7F5EE background, saturated electric cobalt #244CFF, deep blue typography, small acid-citron #D6F045 accent, bright silver. No black background, no warm orange, no beige glass. Lighting: large crisp studio softbox that creates clean highlight ribbons and a directional light-blue shadow; clear tonal separation without murky ambient grey, professional industrial material rendering. Art direction: sophisticated and memorable, modern design-studio launch-film frame, energetic through shape and composition rather than visual clutter. Crisp optical detail. No trademark logos other than original AUREL, no watermarks.
